# PlantFCE Maps walkthrough narration

Use this script for a guided session with an engineer's own delivered workspace. Pause after each step so the participant can repeat the action. This is companion narration, not a verbatim transcript of the introductory video.

## Introduction

In this walkthrough, you will find a piece of equipment, inspect its details, and explore the surrounding P&ID. Open the PlantFCE Maps workspace delivered to your team and choose an equipment tag you recognize.

## Search for equipment

Enter the tag in search and select the matching result. Check the equipment label and the drawing it belongs to. Before moving on, make sure this is the item you intended to inspect.

## Inspect the details

Select the equipment and read its available details. Compare the tag and drawing reference with the P&ID. Keep the original drawing in view as you inspect the information.

## Examine a line

Now select a nearby line. Read the available fields and compare them with the line designation printed on the drawing. Use your project's conventions to interpret that designation.

## Explore the drawing

Pan through the surrounding area and zoom in to read an instrument label or a connection. When you need the wider context again, reset the view.

## Check an off-page reference

Find an off-page connector and inspect its details. Identify the referenced continuation. If your workspace provides a linked destination, follow it using the method covered in your handover and check that the destination matches the reference. Record any connection you cannot resolve for review.

## Finish the exercise

You have practiced finding equipment, reading details, and investigating a drawing reference. Repeat these steps with another equipment tag. You should be able to explain what you selected, which drawing it belongs to, and where you would continue your investigation.
