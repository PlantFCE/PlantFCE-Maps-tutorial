# Practice with your own drawing set

Choose a familiar equipment item in your delivered PlantFCE Maps workspace. Complete the steps below and retain the results in your team's approved internal location.

- [ ] Find the equipment using its tag.
- [ ] Identify the drawing containing the selected equipment.
- [ ] Compare its displayed details with the drawing label.
- [ ] Select a nearby line and read its available details.
- [ ] Match the line details to the designation printed on the P&ID.
- [ ] Pan and zoom to inspect a nearby instrument.
- [ ] Reset the view to recover the broader context.
- [ ] Select an off-page connector and identify its reference.
- [ ] Where a linked continuation is available, follow it using your workspace instructions and check the destination.
- [ ] Record any missing, unclear, or inconsistent information for review.

## Explain what you found

At the end of the exercise, describe the equipment you selected, the line you inspected, and the reference you investigated. Explain which connections you could verify and which still need checking.

If your chosen drawing contains no off-page connector, choose another drawing for that part of the exercise.

## Repeat with a colleague

Ask a colleague to choose another equipment tag from the same workspace. Repeat the exercise and compare your interpretation of the drawing references. Keep the discussion and its supporting drawings within your approved project channels.
