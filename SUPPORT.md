# Support and tutorial feedback

## Questions about this tutorial

Use public repository issues, when enabled, for general wording corrections, unclear steps, or suggestions for additional lessons.

Describe the lesson and the instruction that needs improvement. Use fictional examples rather than customer equipment tags or project identifiers.

## Questions about your workspace

For missing tags, unexpected line details, drawing connections, or delivery questions, contact PlantFCE through the project contact used for your handover. New enquiries can start at the [PlantFCE Maps website](https://maps.plantfce.com/).

Do not attach proprietary P&IDs, workspace files, screenshots, exports, credentials, or customer information to public issues or comments. Arrange any necessary file sharing through your approved project channel.
