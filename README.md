# PlantFCE Maps tutorial for engineers

Learn to find equipment, inspect line details, and explore P&IDs in your delivered PlantFCE Maps workspace. This tutorial uses your own drawing set, so you can practice with equipment and connections you recognize.

[Watch the introductory video on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tt4XC8Ov3UA) · [Visit PlantFCE Maps](https://maps.plantfce.com/)

## Before you start

To follow the exercises, you need access to a delivered PlantFCE Maps workspace and familiarity with the P&IDs it contains. Choose an equipment tag from your drawing set as your starting point.

You can read the guide and watch the video without a workspace. This repository contains documentation only. Plant drawings, customer workspaces, and the PlantFCE Maps application are not distributed here.

## Work through the tutorial

1. [Find equipment and explore a drawing](tutorial/getting-started.md).
2. [Practice with your own drawing set](tutorial/practice.md).
3. [Get help](SUPPORT.md).

For a guided session, use the [walkthrough narration](tutorial/walkthrough-script.md).

The video introduces the workflow. Your tags, available details, drawing connections, and interface may differ from the example. Follow the instructions supplied with your delivered workspace where controls differ.

## Share feedback

Suggestions about the tutorial are welcome. Keep customer drawings, equipment tags, screenshots, exports, and workspace files out of public GitHub discussions and issues. See [Support](SUPPORT.md) for how to ask a question safely.

## About PlantFCE Maps

PlantFCE Maps adds interactive navigation to PDF P&IDs, including tag search, line tracing, and identifiable connections across a submitted drawing set. The [introductory video and its description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tt4XC8Ov3UA) explain the workflow and reviewed delivery process.

Visit [PlantFCE Maps](https://maps.plantfce.com/) for current product and pilot information.
