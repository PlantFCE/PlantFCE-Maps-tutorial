# Find equipment and explore a drawing

This lesson takes you from a known equipment tag to its surrounding lines and off-page references. Work in your own delivered PlantFCE Maps workspace and keep any notes within your approved internal systems.

## Open your workspace

Open the PlantFCE Maps workspace delivered to your team using its supplied instructions. Choose an equipment item you recognize and have its tag ready.

If you do not have a workspace, use the [introductory video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tt4XC8Ov3UA) to observe the workflow. No sample drawing package is required or provided.

## Find equipment by tag

Enter your equipment tag in the search control and select the matching result. Compare the displayed tag and drawing with the equipment you intended to inspect.

The [search segment at approximately 0:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tt4XC8Ov3UA&t=5s) illustrates searching across P&IDs. Use a tag from your own workspace rather than copying an example from the video.

**Check your result:** Can you identify both the equipment and the drawing containing it? If you see no match, check the spelling and whether the relevant drawing belongs to your delivered set.

## Inspect equipment details

Select the equipment to inspect its available details. In the [video at approximately 0:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tt4XC8Ov3UA&t=10s), the Overview panel shows the item type, drawing, and tag.

Compare these details with the original labels visible on your P&ID. Note a discrepancy for review instead of assuming that the displayed metadata resolves it.

**Check your result:** The selected item, its displayed tag, and the drawing label should describe the same equipment.

## Inspect a line

Select a nearby line and read its available details alongside the line designation printed on the drawing. The [video at approximately 0:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tt4XC8Ov3UA&t=25s) shows a line details panel with fields such as size, fluid code, identifier, line code, and insulation code.

The populated fields depend on your drawing and delivered map. Interpret the designation using your project's own conventions.

**Check your result:** Can you match the selected line to its printed designation and explain the information available for it?

## Pan and zoom

Pan through the surrounding drawing and zoom in to read nearby instruments and connection labels. Use the navigation controls supplied in your workspace.

The video demonstrates [pan and zoom around 0:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tt4XC8Ov3UA&t=35s) and [Reset view around 0:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tt4XC8Ov3UA&t=45s). Reset the view when you need to regain the wider drawing context.

**Check your result:** Can you move from an equipment overview to a readable instrument label and then recover the broader view?

## Inspect an off-page reference

Locate an off-page connector and select it to inspect the available reference details. The [video around 0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tt4XC8Ov3UA&t=55s) shows a selected connector and its Overview panel.

Read the reference together with its surrounding line designation. Where your delivered workspace provides a linked continuation, follow it using the instructions supplied during your handover. Compare the source reference and destination drawing to establish that you have reached the intended continuation.

If a reference has no linked destination, check whether the destination drawing is included in your delivered set. Record unresolved references for your team's review or discuss them with PlantFCE through your existing project contact.

**Check your result:** Can you explain where the reference points and whether its continuation is available in your workspace?

## Continue practicing

Repeat the workflow with another equipment item using the [practice checklist](practice.md). The exercise is complete when you can explain what you selected, which drawing it belongs to, and where you would look next.
